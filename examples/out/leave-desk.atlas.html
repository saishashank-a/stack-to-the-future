Time off

How a request for time off is made, approved or declined, and taken from the balance.

Request time off

An employee asks for days off. Depending on length and who is asking, it is approved automatically, by the requester (if a manager), or by their manager. Approved days come off the balance and the employee is told.

Ways in (6)
  • POST /leave api route
  • GET /leave/mine api route
  • POST /leave/{request_id}/approve api route
  • POST /leave/{request_id}/reject api route
  • leave.notify_manager background job
  • leave.notify_decision background job
Files in this part (6)
  • app/leave/models.py Describes what is stored: people, their roles and balances, and each leave request with its status.
  • app/leave/schemas.py Checks the shape of a leave request coming in and decides what is shown back.
  • app/leave/routes.py The front desk for time off: where requests come in and where managers give their answer.
  • app/leave/service.py The rulebook: decides who must approve, applies approvals and refusals, and kicks off emails.
  • app/leave/balance.py Keeps each person's remaining days: checks there are enough and takes approved days off.
  • app/leave/tasks.py Sends the 'please review' and 'here's the decision' emails in the background.

HR reporting

Monthly totals of approved leave for HR, plus a planned spreadsheet export.

Monthly leave summary

On the first of each month (or when HR asks), last month's approved leave is totalled per person and emailed to HR.

Export leave data

HR asks for a spreadsheet of leave for a date range. The screen exists but the export itself is not built yet.

Ways in (3)
  • reports.monthly_summary (1st of month, 06:00) scheduled job
  • POST /reports/monthly/run api route
  • GET /reports/export api route
Files in this part (3)
  • app/reports/routes.py HR's doors into reporting: run the monthly summary now, or ask for an export.
  • app/reports/service.py Adds up approved leave per person and writes it as a summary; the export is still a placeholder.
  • app/reports/tasks.py The monthly job: works out last month, gets the summary and emails it to HR.
